  - type: faq.html
    section_id: faq
    title: How It Works
    items:
      - q: Who teaches the courses?
        a: >-
          I do. I am a solutions architect with 20+ years in software and 10+ on
          AWS, an AWS Authorized Instructor, and I hold certifications
          across AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, including Claude Certified Architect – Professional, Claude Certified
          Developer – Professional and AWS Certified
          Generative AI Developer – Professional. My day job is building production
          AI on Claude and AWS, which is where the course examples and labs come from.
          More on the [home page](/).

      - q: Online or on site?
        a: >-
          Both. Online courses run as live sessions in half-day blocks, which keeps
          attention up and fits around normal work; the AI agents cohort runs as
          two-hour sessions over six weeks. On-site courses run anywhere in
          Europe; travel is charged at cost.

      - q: Can the course be tailored to our team?
        a: >-
          Yes, and it usually is. The outlines above are a starting point: I adjust
          the depth, the examples and the labs to your stack, your use cases and the
          experience of the group. You get a detailed outline to approve before the
          course.

      - q: Do you have practice exams?
        a: >-
          Yes. [CertDrill](https://certdrill.io/) is my practice platform for
          all five Claude exams, with a free practice set for each exam and a
          full practice bank on the paid plan. Each course page links to the
          free set for its exam.

      - q: How much does Claude training cost?
        a: >-
          Team training starts from €4,000 for a course of one to two days,
          excluding VAT and travel; the price depends on length, format and group
          size. Official AWS courses are priced by the AWS Training Partner that
          books them.

      - q: Can you train our team in Croatian?
        a: >-
          Yes. Every course runs in English or Croatian, online or on site, for
          up to 20 participants per group.

      - q: What do participants need for the labs?
        a: >-
          A laptop with a modern browser. The AI agents course needs no coding:
          participants work in claude.ai and a no-code automation tool such as
          n8n or Make. Developer and architect labs also use
          Python or Node.js, VS Code, Git and Claude Code, all free. Labs run on
          Claude API or Amazon Bedrock accounts, ideally your own; I help with the
          setup and estimate the usage cost, which is usually small.

      - q: Do you deliver courses for training companies (white-label)?
        a: >-
          Yes. Training providers and resellers can book me as the trainer for
          their own clients, online or on site, including white-label delivery
          under the provider's name. See
          [for training providers](/training/for-training-providers/).

      - q: Is this an official Anthropic course?
        a: >-
          No. I am an independent trainer, not affiliated with Anthropic; I hold
          Claude Certified Architect – Professional and Developer – Professional and build production systems
          with Claude. The Claude
          courses prepare for the four Claude certification exams, but none of my
          courses are official Anthropic courses. The exams themselves are taken at Pearson VUE;
          candidates register through the Anthropic Partner Academy, which requires
          their company to be a member of the Claude Partner Network.
