Claude certification practice questions (public sample)
https://prlic.io/training/claude-mock-exams/

This page offers 20 practice questions for each of the four Claude
certification exams (Associate, Developer, Architect Foundations, Architect
Professional): 80 questions in total, the same set for every visitor, chosen
to cover every exam domain in proportion to the full question bank.

Modes: Study (answer and explanation after each question) or Exam (timed at
2 minutes per question, 40 minutes for 20, with flags and a per-domain
breakdown at the end). Wrong answers are remembered in your browser until you
answer them correctly. Nothing is sent to a server.

Full-length mock exams (a much larger question bank, with timed practice
tests) are available for teams and course participants: info@prlic.io
