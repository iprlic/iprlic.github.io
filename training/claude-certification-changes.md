    title: Questions about the program changes
    items:
      - q: What changes in the Claude exams on 7 December 2026?
        a: >-
          Associate – Foundations, Developer – Foundations and Architect –
          Professional get new exam guides (version 1.1). Each exam moves to
          70 items, of which 60 are scored, still in 120 minutes. The
          Associate domains are regrouped from seven into six; the other two
          keep their domains with new weights. Architect – Foundations does
          not change.

      - q: Where do I pay for a Claude certification exam now?
        a: >-
          Since 12 November 2026, at Pearson's checkout. You sign in to the
          Anthropic Partner Academy, select Register on the certification
          page, and arrive at Pearson already signed in; the partner discount,
          if any, is applied there and the price is locked when you schedule.

      - q: Is there a Claude Developer – Professional exam?
        a: >-
          Yes. Claude Certified Developer – Professional (CCDV-P) was added in
          November 2026: 120 minutes, 70 items with 60 scored, USD 175, valid
          for 12 months, aimed at engineers who run Claude systems in
          production. Developer – Foundations is recommended but not required.

      - q: How often is this page updated?
        a: >-
          I check Anthropic's sources whenever I update my prep courses and
          when Anthropic announces a change, and add a dated entry. The "Last
          checked" date at the top says when I last looked. I am an
          independent trainer, not Anthropic; for anything that affects your
          booking, the Anthropic Partner Academy is authoritative.
