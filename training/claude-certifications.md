  - type: course-overview.html
    section_id: choose
    title: How to choose
    text: >-
      Pick by what you do every day, not by the level that sounds most
      impressive. Each Professional exam assumes the knowledge of the
      Foundations exam on the same track.
    columns:
      - title: You use Claude, you don't build with it
        text: >-
          Start and usually stop with **Associate – Foundations (CCAO-F)**. It
          tests prompting, checking output, choosing the right Claude feature
          and model, setting up Projects for a team, and using company data
          responsibly. Prep course:
          [Claude for Business Teams](/training/claude-associate/). If you want
          to automate your own processes without code, the
          [Build AI Agents with Claude](/training/build-ai-agents/) course fits
          better than an exam.
      - title: You write code that calls Claude
        text: >-
          Take **Developer – Foundations (CCDV-F)** first: the API, tool use,
          MCP, agents, Claude Code, evals and cost. Prep course:
          [Building with Claude](/training/claude-developer/). Once you run
          Claude systems in production and own their reliability, cost and
          security, move on to **Developer – Professional (CCDV-P)**.
      - title: You design systems and make the trade-offs
        text: >-
          Take **Architect – Foundations (CCAR-F)**: agentic architecture,
          Claude Code configuration, MCP tool design, structured output and
          context management, tested through realistic scenarios. Prep course:
          [Architecting with Claude](/training/claude-architect/). Senior
          architects and tech leads who own whole solutions, including
          governance and stakeholders, continue to **Architect – Professional
          (CCAR-P)** with
          [Advanced Claude Architecture](/training/claude-architect-professional/).
      - title: You are planning certification for a team
        text: >-
          Mixed teams usually split: business staff on CCAO-F, developers on
          CCDV-F, one or two architects on CCAR-F. Check first that your
          company can register: the exams require
          [Claude Partner Network membership](/training/claude-exam-eligibility/).
          If it can't, the courses still work as practical training without the
          exam. [Ask me](mailto:ivan@prlic.io?subject=Claude%20certification%20for%20a%20team)
          which mix fits your team.

  - type: faq.html
    section_id: faq
    background_style: bg-subtle
    title: Questions about the Claude certifications
    items:
      - q: How many Claude certifications are there?
        a: >-
          Five. Three at Foundations level (Associate, Developer and
          Architect) and two at Professional level (Developer and Architect).
          Claude Certified Developer – Professional (CCDV-P) is the newest; its
          exam guide was first published in November 2026.

      - q: Which Claude certification is the easiest?
        a: >-
          Associate – Foundations (CCAO-F) is the entry point: it needs no
          coding and costs USD 99. All five exams have the same length (120
          minutes) and the same passing score (720 out of 1,000), so the
          difference is in the content, not the format. The two Professional
          exams assume production experience.

      - q: Do I need a Foundations certification before a Professional one?
        a: >-
          No. None of the exams has a mandatory prerequisite. Anthropic
          recommends Developer – Foundations before Developer – Professional,
          and Architect – Foundations before Architect – Professional.

      - q: What changes on 7 December 2026?
        a: >-
          New exam guides take effect for Associate – Foundations, Developer –
          Foundations and Architect – Professional. Each exam moves to 70
          items, of which 60 are scored, still in 120 minutes. The Associate
          domains are regrouped from seven into six; Developer – Foundations
          and Architect – Professional keep their domains with new weights.
          Architect – Foundations is unchanged. My prep courses are reviewed
          against the new guides before any delivery after that date. The
          [program changes log](/training/claude-certification-changes/) has
          the domain-by-domain detail.

      - q: How long is a Claude certification valid?
        a: >-
          12 months from the date it is awarded. According to the exam guides,
          on-time renewal is a free, non-proctored assessment on the Anthropic
          Partner Academy; if the certification lapses, you retake the full
          exam at the full fee.

      - q: Who can take the exams?
        a: >-
          Registration goes through the Anthropic Partner Academy, which
          requires the candidate's company to be a member of the Claude
          Partner Network. See
          [who can take the Claude certification exams](/training/claude-exam-eligibility/)
          for the details and the options if your company is not a member.

      - q: Is this guide official?
        a: >-
          No. I am an independent trainer who holds Anthropic's Claude
          certifications and builds production systems with Claude. This
          comparison is my own summary of Anthropic's public exam guides, and
          my courses are not official Anthropic courses. For the authoritative
          details, read the exam guide on each certification's page in the
          Anthropic Partner Academy.
