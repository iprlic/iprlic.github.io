    title: Eligibility and registration
    text: >-
      Anthropic sets the rules, and they can change. The authoritative source
      is Anthropic's
      [certification eligibility page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/certification-eligibility);
      this page summarises it together with the exam guides.
    columns:
      - title: Who can register
        items:
          - People who work at a company that is a member of the Claude Partner Network
          - Registration happens on the Anthropic Partner Academy, so you need access to it through your company
          - "There are no mandatory prerequisites: no required course and no earlier certification"
          - On exam day you need a valid, government-issued photo ID whose name matches your registration exactly
      - title: How registration works
        items:
          - Open the certification's page on the Anthropic Partner Academy and read its exam guide
          - Register for the exam and continue to Pearson VUE
          - Choose online proctoring or a Pearson VUE test centre, then a date and time
          - Pay at checkout; the price reflects any partner discount
          - Results, receipts and rescheduling are handled in your Pearson VUE account
    note: >-
      Not sure whether your company is a member? Ask whoever manages your
      company's relationship with Anthropic. Questions about registration go to
      Anthropic certification support (certifications-support@anthropic.com);
      scheduling and exam-day questions go to
      [Pearson VUE](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html).

  - type: faq.html
    section_id: faq
    background_style: bg-subtle
    title: Questions about exam eligibility
    items:
      - q: Can an individual take a Claude certification exam?
        a: >-
          Only through an eligible company. Registration goes through the
          Anthropic Partner Academy, which requires the candidate's company to
          be a member of the Claude Partner Network. Check Anthropic's
          [eligibility page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/certification-eligibility)
          for the current rules.

      - q: Where are the exams taken?
        a: >-
          At Pearson VUE, either online with a remote proctor (OnVUE) or at a
          Pearson VUE test centre. All five exams last 120 minutes.

      - q: How much do the exams cost?
        a: >-
          List prices from the exam guides: Associate – Foundations USD 99,
          Developer – Foundations and Architect – Foundations USD 125 each,
          Developer – Professional and Architect – Professional USD 175 each.
          The price at checkout reflects any partner discount, and each retake
          is paid again. See the
          [comparison of all five certifications](/training/claude-certifications/).

      - q: Can my company book your course if it is not a Claude partner?
        a: >-
          Yes. My courses are open to any company; only the exam has the
          partner requirement. Participants learn the same content and can take
          the exam later if their company becomes eligible.

      - q: Can a training provider deliver your Claude courses?
        a: >-
          Yes. Training companies and academies can book me to deliver the
          courses to their own clients; see
          [for training providers](/training/for-training-providers/). The exam
          rules stay the same: whether a participant can register depends on
          their own company's membership.

      - q: Are you affiliated with Anthropic?
        a: >-
          No. I am an independent trainer who holds Anthropic's Claude
          certifications and builds production systems with Claude. This page
          summarises public information; it is not an official Anthropic page,
          and my courses are not official Anthropic courses.
