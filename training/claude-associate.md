      One of four [Claude courses](/training/), for people who use Claude as a

    columns:
      - title: Who it's for
        items:
          - Operations, marketing, HR, finance, project management and client services teams
          - Consultants and communications staff who draft, research and summarise every day
          - Companies that have just rolled out Claude and want everyone using it the same, safe way
          - Anyone preparing for the Claude Certified Associate – Foundations exam
      - title: What you'll be able to do
        items:
          - Pick the right Claude feature and model for a task, and know when to start a fresh chat
          - Turn a vague request into a clear prompt, then iterate it into a usable result
          - Check output for errors, invented facts and bias, and know when a person must review it
          - Redesign a real workflow around Claude and explain its value and limits to colleagues
          - Set up a Project with instructions and a knowledge base the whole team can rely on
          - Work with sensitive data inside company policy, and fix weak or inconsistent results

  - type: course-modules.html
    section_id: modules
    title: Course outline
    text: >-
      Each module opens with a request from a fictional events agency, works
      through exam-style scenarios and a demo, and closes with a knowledge check
      and a lab in claude.ai.
    modules:
      - title: Claude Platform and Model Selection
        text: >-
          Chat, Projects, Research and artifacts, and when to reach for each.
          Haiku, Sonnet and Opus weighed against cost, speed and quality. Keeping
          long work on track with context and memory.
        lab: Pick the tool, pick the model
      - title: Prompting and Task Execution
        text: >-
          What makes a prompt work, breaking a complex request into steps, and
          iterating for analysis, research, drafting and brainstorming.
        lab: Prompt a venue brief
      - title: Evaluating and Validating Output
        text: >-
          Accuracy and completeness, hallucinations, inconsistencies and bias,
          fact-checking, when human review is required, and adapting output to
          its audience.
        lab: Validate and adapt a contract summary
      - title: Workflow Integration and Solution Design
        text: >-
          Finding good use cases, designing Claude into a workflow rather than
          bolting it on, and knowing when a job needs a developer or architect.
        lab: Redesign a workflow
      - title: Configuration and Knowledge Management
        text: >-
          Projects with instructions and knowledge, uploads and connectors,
          memory and Skills, and keeping a shared setup current.
        lab: Configure a Project with a policy knowledge base
      - title: Governance, Risk and Responsible Use
        text: >-
          Appropriate and inappropriate use, data sensitivity and privacy,
          anonymising before analysis, and working within company policy.
        lab: Governance decisions
      - title: Troubleshooting and Optimization
        text: >-
          Diagnosing poor results, adjusting from feedback, and making a
          workflow faster and more consistent.
        lab: Troubleshoot and optimize
      - title: Summary and Exam Readiness
        text: >-
          A recap of all seven domains, an exam-style practice set, what to
          expect on exam day, and a personal study plan.
