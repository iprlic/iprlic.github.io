# Privacy notice

Last updated: 26 November 2026

## Who is responsible

Prlić Consulting d.o.o. za usluge, Malogorička ulica 42, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia, OIB 84570551730, is the controller for personal data collected through this website. Contact: [info@prlic.io](mailto:info@prlic.io).

## What we collect and why

- **E-mail enquiries.** If you write to us, we receive your name, e-mail address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and, if we work together, to prepare and perform the contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
- **Call bookings.** Booking a call through Calendly shares your name, e-mail address, the chosen time and any answers you give with us. Calendly LLC processes this on our behalf under its own terms and may transfer data to the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
- **Visit statistics.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying or tracking individual visitors.
- **Hosting and fonts.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare; both process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. Our legitimate interest is running a secure, working website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

This website sets no cookies, has no newsletter or tracking for advertising, and we never sell personal data.

## How long we keep it

Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry or our business relationship, and longer only where accounting and tax law require it.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Write to [info@prlic.io](mailto:info@prlic.io). You can also complain to the Croatian data protection authority, Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka ([azop.hr](https://azop.hr)).
